🔑 Extract Public Key from MetaMask

This tool helps you extract your Ethereum public key from MetaMask so you can create multisig accounts without exposing your private key. The public key is derived from your signature of a simple message.

Step 1: Connect Wallet

Connect your MetaMask wallet to get started.

Step 2: Extract Public Key

Sign a simple message to extract your public key. This doesn't send any transaction or spend any gas—it's just used to mathematically derive your public key from the signature.

💡 Next Steps for Multisig

After each participant imports their public key, create the multisig with:
shardeumd keys add mymulti \
  --multisig alice-pub,bob-pub,carol-pub \
  --multisig-threshold 2 \
  --keyring-backend test
