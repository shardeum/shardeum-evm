🦊 Delegate Stake via MetaMask

💡 About EIP-712 on Shardeum:
This demo shows how to use MetaMask to sign Cosmos staking transactions on Shardeum using the EIP-712 standard. This allows users with MetaMask to perform staking operations without needing Keplr or other Cosmos wallets.
⚠️ Important - MetaMask Network: You MUST switch MetaMask to the Shardeum network before signing! Click "Add Shardeum Local Network" button below and switch to it in MetaMask.

The manual Cosmos Chain ID field is for the transaction content, but MetaMask must be on chain 8117 (local) or 8118 (mainnet).
